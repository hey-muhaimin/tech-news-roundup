### Five Emerging Tech Trends
* Multiagent Coding: Industry shifts toward autonomous agent swarms that collaborate on complex software development tasks [6].
* Model Distillation Restrictions: Labs are increasingly implementing safeguards to prevent smaller models from being trained on the outputs of superior systems [1][3].
* Orbital Computing: Companies are exploring space-based infrastructure to meet the massive energy and compute demands of future AI models [1][2].
* Independent Energy Projects: AI firms are pursuing utility-scale, self-contained power solutions—such as gas plants—to bypass grid constraints [5].
* Policy-Driven Integration: Big Tech is formalizing agentic access via standard protocols like MCP, while enforcing strict administrative audit requirements [3][5].
