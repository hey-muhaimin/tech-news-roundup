### Five Emerging Tech Trends

* Agentic Governance: Companies are moving beyond simple AI automation to delegating authority—such as PR approvals and purchase authorizations—to AI agents [1][2][3].
* Perplexity-Based Safety: Industry leaders are shifting toward mathematical pretraining thresholds, such as log-perplexity, to create objective safety benchmarks for frontier models [2].
* Subscription-Free Automotive Rights: Regulatory shifts, exemplified by new New York legislation, are beginning to restrict automakers from charging post-purchase subscription fees for preinstalled hardware features [4].
* Autonomous Transit Scaling: Driverless robotaxi testing is expanding rapidly, with major firms like Uber, Nuro, and Lucid initiating public road trials ahead of 2027 commercial launches [3][4].
* Independent Browsing Engines: The release of the Ladybird browser alpha marks a move toward de-monetized, independent engines that challenge the dominance of Chromium and WebKit [2].
